# Build 5C — Growth Funnel and Revenue Engine

Growth Engine by 1 Minute Manager. Acceptance date: 2026-10-03. This remains a local single-browser prototype; no external analytics or backend was introduced.

1. **Built:** customizable funnel builder, period entry/history, conversion and constraint analysis, revenue backsolve, temporary scenarios, and links to existing Growth work. Routes: `/scale/funnel`, `/scale/funnel/builder`, `/scale/funnel/history`.
2. **Architecture:** pure domain functions and mutations in `lib/scale/funnel/{model,logic,mutations,validation}.ts`; focused UI modules for workspace/context, builder/periods and revenue/scenarios. Existing Scale provider handles persistence.
3. **Templates:** optional editable Consulting, B2B Sales, E-commerce and Import/Distribution templates, plus an empty custom structure. Business model metadata supports all eleven requested categories. An explicit educational sample creates a draft with 300/120/60/30/8/3 volumes, a 100M average contract and 1.5B target; it never masquerades as actual company data or silently completes a period.
4. **Stages:** stable IDs, ordered names/types, descriptions, units, role owners, target volume/conversion and active state. Stages can be added, renamed, reordered or retired. Retiring the configured revenue stage is blocked until the revenue model is changed. Only one funnel is primary; old funnels and stages remain available historically.
5. **Measurements:** weekly/monthly/custom draft periods contain dates and per-stage volume, optional entered revenue, source and notes. Missing volumes remain null. Explicit completion requires all active stage volumes and at least two stages; future periods cannot be completed. Completion freezes funnel, stage and revenue definitions.
6. **Conversion:** current volume / previous volume × 100. Missing or zero predecessor yields unknown; overflow never yields Infinity/NaN. An observed value above 100% is displayed with a cohort/period warning, is not considered healthy, and cannot drive a sequential scenario.
7. **Drop-off:** previous minus current volume, plus the percentage of previous volume. A negative drop-off remains visible as a data/model issue rather than being hidden.
8. **Gaps:** actual minus target conversion in percentage points. Missing targets remain unknown. Targets above 100% or negative/non-finite values are rejected.
9. **Constraint:** internal weighted signal combines relative conversion gap (35%), relative absolute drop-off (30%), recoverable downstream revenue implication (20%) and conversion deterioration (15%). Available weights are normalized; unknown conversion remains unknown. Categories are low/medium/high/critical. This is a prioritization heuristic, not proof of causation. It does not merely rank lowest conversion. The primary Growth Constraint is never automatically overwritten.
10. **Health:** stage performance uses actual versus internal target or prior conversion, with owner/freshness cautions. Funnel health uses conversion 50%, trend stability 20%, target coverage 10%, freshness 10%, owners 10%; missing components are excluded and coverage is shown. Without conversion performance the aggregate stays unknown. This is not overall Scale Health.
11. **Trends:** completed periods display volume, conversion and deltas with no fabricated backfill; changed predecessor IDs/units suppress invalid conversion comparisons. Period history shows revenue estimates, entered actual revenue and constraint movement. Unequal-length/overlapping periods are explicitly cautioned; raw volume comparisons are not normalized for seasonality or exposure.
12. **Revenue:** managers explicitly choose the revenue stage and currency. Base estimate = revenue-stage volume × average deal/order value. Entered actual revenue, repeat rate and expansion revenue are separate metadata captured at completion, not silently added to the base estimate. Revenue-stage measurements also retain optional entered amounts separately.
13. **Backsolve:** target / average value, rounded up to required revenue-stage volume, then recursively divided by each positive conversion and rounded up. Current conversions are default; target conversions are selectable. Missing, zero or invalid rates leave upstream requirements unknown. Current structure must match the historical transition before a rate is reused.
14. **Revenue gap:** target minus estimated output and target/current customer volumes are shown. Volume, conversion, value and repeat/expansion are possible interacting levers; the app does not prescribe one automatically.
15. **Scenarios:** temporary top-stage volume, selected conversion assumptions, average value and additional repeat rate calculate conditional output. Assumptions are component state only; reload discards them. Repeat explicitly means one additional same-value purchase for the chosen fraction and warns against double-counting. No probability or forecasting claim; optional saved scenarios were not implemented.
16. **Constraints:** any reported transition can append immutable evidence (period, stage names, actual, target, gap) to an existing constraint or create a candidate. The link and frozen evidence appear on its detail page. Existing primary selection is preserved.
17. **Ideas:** optional stage association is managed from idea detail; related ideas appear in the stage workspace.
18. **Experiments:** optional stage association is managed from experiment detail and stored in Scale's relation table. Started/closed experiment links are frozen. Context shows latest completed period conversion and trend, explicitly without attribution. Old Build 5B records need no migration rewrite. Stage links are independent metadata; new revisions can be associated explicitly.
19. **Strategy:** numeric KR target can be selected read-only. The selected source must exist and its unit must exactly match the model currency; otherwise target remains unknown with an explanation. No currency/scale conversion or Strategy write occurs. Managers must verify period alignment. Unit-match and missing-source behavior are covered by tests.
20. **Capacity:** a required output increase of at least 50% (or positive requirement from zero output) displays System process health, independence and dependency evidence. It does not hard-block planning or pretend missing capacity is safe.
21. **Completeness/freshness:** definitions, owner coverage, period volumes, targets and revenue setup are checked. Freshness uses the latest completed period and configurable 1–365 day threshold. Attention includes missing/stale data, ownership gaps, conversion gaps/decline, unlinked primary evidence, experiments without stage links and absent average value. Capacity pressure is shown in the revenue section.
22. **Persistence:** optional `funnelData` extends `growth-engine.scale.v1`; previous stores remain valid and unchanged until an explicit action. Runtime validation checks shapes, IDs, references, finite values and history. The general persistence path enforces completed-period/measurement/evidence immutability, retained funnel/stage IDs and frozen experiment links. Browser origin isolation and existing optimistic-write checks remain. No server backup or multi-user guarantee.
23. **Tests:** 433 passing tests (401 existing + 32 funnel tests). Requested A–X plus malformed data, missing volumes, overflow, removal/history, changed predecessor, incompatible currency and invalid observed conversion. One earlier concurrent test/build run exceeded the existing dashboard performance time limit; isolated rerun and then the entire suite passed without changing the limit. Production build includes TypeScript validation.
24. **Browser QA:** on localhost, explicitly created the educational funnel, assigned roles, added/reordered/removed an unsaved test stage, completed two periods, verified conversion/drop-off and 800M then 1B estimates. Current-rate backsolve required 500 leads for 15 contracts; target-rate backsolve required 380. A temporary scenario of 500 leads and 40% contract conversion produced 2B; refresh restored actual scenario inputs (330 leads) without altering periods. Linked constraint evidence and an idea, created a separate EXP-3 draft and linked it to Contract. System capacity warning was displayed. Additional final responsive/console checks are recorded below.
25. **Dependencies:** none added. Existing Next.js/React, UI primitives and Node test infrastructure are reused.
26. **Issues/limits:** early JSX syntax/type errors were fixed before the successful build. Browser tooling lost bindings during interruption and required reconnecting; the local server also required restart. Results are manual cohort/period observations, not causal attribution or accounting revenue. Period entry is intentionally explicit; historical corrections require a new period. Velocity is deferred because no individual transition timestamps exist.
27. **Build 5D:** growth loops, final Scale dashboard, channel attribution, retention cohorts, advanced CAC/LTV, forecasting, AI, external analytics, backend and authentication remain outside this build. Exact next-build scope should follow a separate request.

Final browser acceptance: six pages at 390/820/1440 widths had no document overflow. Desktop, mobile and tablet screenshots were visually checked; mobile stage cards remained readable, with keyboard focus visible on evidence controls. Invalid 101% scenario conversion showed an error. Selecting a non-currency KR produced an unknown revenue target and clear unit-mismatch explanation; no source data was changed. The existing localhost dataset has no matching revenue KR, so the positive populated source case is verified by the pure integration test rather than fabricated Strategy data. EXP-3's Contract link survived reload. Both completed periods and fixed constraint evidence were rechecked after restart. No captured console warnings/errors or hydration errors were found. Seven routes and 22 referenced Next assets returned HTTP 200. Production build/TypeScript passed and the app runs at http://localhost:3000/scale/funnel. Temporary viewport override was reset after QA. Data mutations remained on localhost; the separate 127.0.0.1 browser store was not changed.
