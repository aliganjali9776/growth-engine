# Build 5A — Scale foundation

Growth Engine by 1 Minute Manager · 2026-09-30

## Completion report

1. **What was built.** Scale management home, structured diagnosis, constraint list/detail/editor, reusable lever library with custom capture, and growth idea list/detail with quick capture and enrichment. Routes: `/scale`, `/scale/diagnosis`, `/scale/constraints`, `/scale/constraints/[id]`, `/scale/levers`, `/scale/ideas`, `/scale/ideas/[id]`. Experiments, funnel, loops and reports have explicitly labelled future-state pages without fabricated metrics. Existing main navigation is reused.

2. **Scale Profile.** Stores business model, descriptive growth stage, growth goal/horizon, optional current/target revenue, primary metric, notes and timestamps. Eleven models are supported: service, consulting, e-commerce, retail, B2B, SaaS/subscription, manufacturing, distribution/import, education, marketplace and custom. Assessment's existing business model is displayed as source evidence; company identity/team profile is not copied. A selected Strategy KR is an ID relationship, not a duplicate target. Linking it clears independent goal/revenue target fields in the form. Sources and values remain owned by Strategy.

3. **Growth Diagnosis model.** Ten `GrowthDimensionDiagnostic` records hold status, evidence, optional metric value, comparison basis, management observation, evidence completeness and timestamp. A concise manual metric section includes period, currency/unit, leads, qualified leads, opportunities, customers, repeat customers, revenue, average value, known rates, sales/delivery capacity and founder involvement. Missing numbers remain `null`; measured zero remains zero. Percentage fields are constrained to 0–100. Channel mix is explicit management text with its measurement basis.

4. **Growth Dimension logic.** Dimensions are acquisition, initial conversion, sales conversion, retention/repeat, revenue expansion, channel concentration, sales capacity, delivery capacity, team capacity and founder dependency. Non-unknown labels require evidence or a management observation. A candidate is an explicit management classification with recorded support, not an automatic root-cause claim. Candidate creation is a deliberate action. Comparison bases are internal target, company history/trend or management judgment. Model-specific guidance distinguishes subscriptions, marketplace sides, order/repeat purchasing, production/distribution capacity and service/B2B contracts. No external benchmark is supplied. Per-dimension completeness gives 50% for evidence and 50% for an observation or metric; setup completeness separately reports six labelled checks.

5. **Constraint architecture.** `GrowthConstraint` separates the problem title/description, observed symptom, dimension, evidence, severity, growth impact, confidence, responsible role, lifecycle, links, levers and notes. Detail pages expose connected ideas and source links. Lifecycle: candidate, active, monitoring, resolved, archived. Active constraints require a responsible role ID. Resolved records receive a resolution timestamp. Archive preserves the record and links; it is excluded from active counts.

6. **Primary Constraint logic.** A single nullable `primaryConstraintId` references an active constraint. Selecting another replaces this one ID without deleting either record. Candidate/monitoring/resolved/archived constraints cannot be primary. Moving the primary out of active clears the selection. Both mutation and persistence validation enforce the invariant.

7. **Evidence model.** Typed evidence supports metric, observation, System finding, Strategy finding, customer feedback and other. Strong structural quality requires a non-empty title, impact, evidence and an owner when active. Missing evidence is shown separately from incomplete structure. Severity is independent of confidence; confidence describes the evidence behind the diagnosis, not certainty or probability. The software does not infer root cause from a symptom.

8. **Growth Lever model.** Twenty-five seeded levers cover content, paid acquisition, partnerships, referral, outbound, events, offer, pricing, follow-up, sales conversation, proof, response speed, onboarding, customer success, repeat purchase, loyalty, quality, upsell, cross-sell, product mix, contract size, hiring, delegation, automation and process redesign. Levers have stable IDs, categories, applicable dimensions, descriptions, active/custom flags and creation time. Constraint suggestions use dimension matching and are explicitly framework guidance. Management may add a custom lever. The home's highlighted lever is the linked lever with most open ideas; ties use stable IDs. It is not an effectiveness recommendation.

9. **Idea Backlog model.** `GrowthIdea` supports title, description, source, constraint/lever links, owner, rationale/intervention logic, expected impact, confidence, effort, cost metadata, evidence, success-metric concept, lifecycle and status history. Fast capture needs only title and source; an inbox idea may be unlinked and unrated. List search, status/constraint filters and recency/priority sorting are available. A board was optional and was not added. Inbox/review/prioritized/ready/rejected/archived are usable. Experiment and validated transitions are rejected in validation and omitted from editing controls.

10. **Idea prioritization logic.** Internal deterministic score: impact × confidence × effort factor. Impact/confidence low/medium/high map to 1/2/3; effort low/medium/high maps to 3/2/1. Categories: 18–27 very strong candidate, 10–17 suitable candidate, 5–9 needs more evidence, 1–4 low priority. Unknown impact/confidence/effort produces no score. UI emphasizes category and exposes the rule in a disclosure. Low effort improves priority. No probability, ROI estimate or causal confidence is claimed.

11. **Idea Readiness.** Prioritized/ready transitions require an open linked constraint, active lever or explicit intervention logic, rationale, known impact/confidence/effort and a success-metric concept. Owner is optional at this stage. Missing fields appear in a readable error and prevent the transition. If a related constraint is later resolved/archived, its idea remains historically linked, readiness is recalculated as false and Needs Attention flags the stale ready state. Ready counts include only ready-state ideas still satisfying the rules. Readiness means the structure is sufficient to design an experiment; no experiment is created.

12. **Strategy integration.** Existing validated Strategy data is read without writing. Active cycle, non-cancelled Objectives and active KRs are available. Selectors label the cycle and lifecycle to distinguish identical titles across historical/new cycles. Constraint KR selection sets the corresponding Objective; conflicting pairs cannot be saved through the editor. Missing sources show a fallback rather than reseeding Strategy. Growth relevance is explicitly selected by management; there is no keyword classifier claiming which KR is a growth goal. The profile displays live source current/target/unit and, for increase KRs, the remaining difference.

13. **System integration.** Existing validated roles/processes/maps/analysis/standards/measurement data is read. When a map exists, canonical `processHealth` and founder-independence logic calculate displayed evidence. Missing evidence stays unknown. Capacity attention is a management screening rule: high/critical founder dependency recorded in the process profile, computed health below 60 or computed independence below 50. The exact reason is displayed, keeping profile declarations distinct from calculated map evidence. These are internal caution rules, not measured throughput or industry capacity benchmarks. Risk does not block idea creation. Assessment Scale score is shown only for a completed valid assessment.

14. **Scale Readiness formula.** Preparation checklist, not Scale Health: diagnostic evidence 20%; primary clarity 25%; goal clarity 15%; primary lever coverage 15%; linked open idea pipeline 15%; System capacity evidence availability 10%. Diagnostic credit is the fraction of ten non-unknown dimensions with evidence. Primary credit is full for strong structure, half for incomplete/evidence-needing primary, zero without a primary. Other components are binary checks. System availability can come from computed health/independence or a recorded non-none founder dependency; it does not mean capacity is healthy. Missing evidence earns no setup credit but is never turned into zero business performance. Breakdown is visible. No score is persisted as an aggregate cache.

15. **Needs Attention.** Flags absent/inaccessible goal, low setup completeness, missing primary, active constraints without evidence, critical constraints with low confidence, primary without levers or open ideas, ready ideas whose prerequisites are no longer valid, disconnected Strategy goal and System capacity risk. Findings link to their management context. A large inbox alone creates no overload warning.

16. **Persistence and architecture.** Independent key `growth-engine.scale.v1`; typed models, seed library, pure logic, validation/mutations and source readers live under `lib/scale`. Client workspaces under `components/scale` share one ScaleProvider. Runtime validation checks enums, text, numeric bounds, finite values, timestamps, unique IDs, dimensions and internal relationships. Only an absent Scale key offers the labelled consulting sample; malformed data is preserved and blocks loading rather than being silently replaced. Explicit saves use compare-and-swap against the last read value and commit React state after successful storage writes. Quota failure leaves the previous value unchanged. Cross-tab changes request reload. Source readers only receive `getItem`; the writer can write only the Scale key. This is local browser storage, not a server-backed or transactional multi-user system. No automatic reset/deletion or import/export recovery workflow was added.

17. **Tests.** Final suite: **368 passed, 0 failed** (343 existing + 25 Scale tests). New tests cover requested A–T: profile persistence, unknown values, candidate creation, unique primary, quality/evidence, independent confidence/severity, lever links, lightweight/unlinked inbox, readiness, priority/effort, correct links, source integration and absent/corrupt sources, readiness missing data, archive counts, roundtrip and unchanged external keys. Additional tests cover future-stage guards, corrupt/duplicate/non-finite/negative input, stale writers, quota failure and large inbox non-warning. TypeScript (`npx.cmd tsc --noEmit`) and production build passed.

18. **Browser QA.** Production server started successfully at `http://127.0.0.1:3000`. Test mutations were confined to `http://localhost:3000/scale` through UI; no localStorage editing. Set B2B model, independent goal, measurement period and 100 leads/12 qualified/3 customers; kept retention unknown. Added diagnostic evidence, created a candidate, assigned sales owner, activated and marked primary, linked an existing Strategy KR and System qualification process, selected content/referral levers. Captured an unlinked inbox idea, demonstrated the incomplete ready-state rejection, enriched it with all prerequisites and saved ready with very-strong category. Profile was then linked to the existing KR and showed 86 current / 100 target / 14 remaining without editing Strategy. Refresh preserved profile, null metrics, idea links and status history. Custom lever capture, nested routes, future-state page and missing-idea recovery were checked. Tab into the editor and Escape worked. Geometry checks covered seven principal pages at 390, 820 and 1440 widths: no document overflow. Representative screenshots covered mobile idea/home, tablet diagnosis and desktop lever/home. No captured console warnings/errors or hydration errors. Eleven routes and nineteen referenced Next assets returned HTTP 200. This is targeted browser QA, not exhaustive device or assistive-technology certification.

19. **Dependencies.** No dependency was added or upgraded. Reused Next/React, existing shared Radix drawer and form components, Vazirmatn and the navy/gold/white RTL shell. Added a Scale TypeScript test configuration and included its tests in `npm.cmd test`.

20. **Issues encountered.** Initial Scale test fixture imports pointed at seed modules outside that test compiler's emitted dependency graph; corrected imports to existing compiled fixture locations. Browser review exposed indistinguishable KR titles across cycles and vague System risk text; added cycle/lifecycle labels and explicit evidence reasons. One browser assertion raced storage hydration; the subsequent rendered form and console check confirmed successful recovery. Final suite/build and browser checks passed. No security policies were modified. Remaining limits: manually classified diagnosis; no automatically inferred industry benchmarks, funnel or root cause; localStorage capacity/concurrency limits; no authentication/backup/sync; preparation scores are heuristic and sample records are clearly labelled.

21. **Build 5B boundary.** The foundation is ready for a separately specified experiment build: hypothesis, success/failure criteria, baseline/measurement period, explicit launch/stop decisions, evidence collection, learning and a guarded validation transition. Full experiment measurement, A/B testing, funnel analytics, growth loops, final Scale dashboard/reports, AI, external analytics, backend and auth remain unimplemented by design. Build 5A does not automatically start those features.
