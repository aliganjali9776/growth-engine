# BUILD 5F — Scale executive dashboard and reporting

Verified locally on 2026-10-04. Application: `/scale/dashboard`. Reporting center: `/scale/reports`. Meeting view: `/scale/reports/executive`.

1. **Architecture.** Read-only selectors in `lib/scale/dashboard/selectors.ts` consume the existing Scale store and System/Strategy source adapter. The dashboard, focused reports and meeting view share selectors. No dashboard store, migration, synthetic history or external service was introduced.
2. **Final health formula.** Constraint clarity 10%, experiment outcomes 20%, funnel conversion 25%, repeated channel demand 15%, operational capacity 20%, learning discipline 10%. The score is `round(sum(available score × weight) / sum(available weight))`. Coverage is displayed separately. Missing components stay unknown; below 60% coverage the status explicitly withholds judgment.
3. **Overlap audit and transparency.** The suggested nine-component formula would reuse demand, conversion and capacity through composite candidate readiness, funnel health and revenue. These composite measures, revenue and loop evidence are therefore displayed separately instead of added again. The six lenses are not claimed to be statistically independent. The expandable breakdown exposes score, weight, normalized contribution and rationale. Status bands follow the requested boundaries; health is neither candidate readiness nor setup readiness and does not authorize scaling.
4. **Growth evidence.** Descriptive states use positive/partial completed experiments, independently positive channel periods and canonical loop strength. Counts of unfinished experiments do not constitute evidence. This is a descriptive heuristic, not causal proof.
5. **Momentum.** Positive/stable/negative/insufficient, based on comparable funnel movement, actual revenue movement, channel movement and comparable readiness snapshots. At least two signals are required. Snapshot comparisons require the same scenario ID, factor and coverage. No exact momentum score or forecast.
6. **Executive summary.** Deterministic text names the primary growth constraint, first capacity risk and highest-ranked management issue. Its source links lead to the existing workflows.
7. **Management attention.** Findings include severe constraints, conversion deterioration, material revenue gaps, stale/unfinished experiment records, repeated negative outcomes, ready ideas without experiments, concentration, weak scaling evidence, loop leakage, capacity/founder risks, blocked preparation, unsupported scale decisions and stale data. Ranking uses ordered severity, capacity, impact, constraint relevance, revenue, evidence, duration and alignment signals. Duplicate links/titles are merged; six findings are shown.
8. **Constraint reporting.** Uses the current active/monitoring primary record, secondary constraints, evidence, severity, confidence and existing links. Movement is only shown when comparable funnel evidence is linked; descriptions are not analyzed to invent movement.
9. **Growth lab.** Shows idea/ready/running/completed/decision inventory and a 30-day learning window. The pipeline is explicitly distinguished from a sales funnel.
10. **Experiment reporting.** Result distribution, completion counts, recorded learnings, duration and missing result/learning/decision attention. Negative results can still count toward learning discipline. Owner, constraint, result and status filters narrow the detail list; the report explicitly labels pipeline/distribution as the whole-portfolio summary.
11. **Funnel reporting.** Uses Build 5C `stageRows`, constraint signal and `healthSummary`, rather than a competing funnel-health formula. Shows volumes, conversion, targets, gaps and movement. The selected transition history excludes incompatible stage/previous-stage/unit definitions.
12. **Revenue reporting.** Actual revenue, funnel estimate and target are separate. Gap identifies its actual-or-estimate basis. Observed-rate and target-rate scenarios use canonical backsolve. Saved Build 5E scenarios use canonical scenario requirements; the saved factor applies to observed output volume, not silently to the revenue target. Currency and conditional-calculation labels remain visible. Volume, conversion, average value and repeat/expansion form an evidence bridge, not causal attribution.
13. **Channels.** Shared-period qualified demand is visualized before raw leads. Portfolio includes maturity, customers, conversion, attributed revenue, optional spend, movement and canonical channel readiness/coverage. Missing spend does not produce an efficiency ratio. Missing demand is labeled, not drawn as zero.
14. **Concentration.** Canonical Build 5D top-one/top-two concentration and resilience, using primary attribution and a common period. Partial coverage is disclosed. The concentration figure remains a whole-portfolio measure when the owner filter narrows the visible channel list.
15. **Loops.** Canonical strength, measured output, metric/unit, cycle time, movement, evidence and unresolved leakage. The selected loop is shown as ordered operational nodes, with idea/experiment links. No decorative infinite loop.
16. **Candidate portfolio.** Source, evidence, readiness, confidence, analytical gate, primary breakpoint and last management decision, with the candidate's most recently recorded scenario identified explicitly.
17. **Gate reporting.** Ready/prepare/hold/insufficient distributions reuse Build 5E assessments. They do not change recorded management decisions.
18. **Growth/capacity matrix.** Uses Build 5E matrix positions, with unknown candidates outside the four known quadrants. Candidate names link to evidence and decisions.
19. **Capacity.** Existing plans supply current capacity/load, scenario requirement, gap and utilization. Units remain separate. The report's candidate/owner/gate filters also scope primary risk and preparation counts.
20. **Breakpoints.** Canonical risks are ranked by severity and relative gap, with evidence and proposed preparation. Absence of a risk is not represented as proof of adequate capacity.
21. **Next constraint.** Each candidate's canonical next constraint remains visible with scenario evidence, rather than a prediction of when it will occur.
22. **System integration.** Only processes linked to candidates contribute the displayed health, standardization, adoption, independence and founder-dependency context. System records are not modified.
23. **Strategy integration.** Candidate/objective/priority/KR context is read-only. Objective links use the existing cycle-specific route. Major scaling commitments without a valid objective/KR link are flagged; exploratory work is not automatically rejected.
24. **3S connection.** Direction, growth mechanism and organizational capacity are presented together with real linked candidate/objective/process records. A saved link alone is not asserted to prove alignment.
25. **Data quality.** Ten explicit completeness checks, latest funnel/channel/measurement/assessment dates, freshness thresholds and low/medium/high confidence. Confidence combines completeness, freshness, channel history depth and documented learning. It is separate from business performance. Early-stage users receive a three-step evidence-building entry point and unavailable analytical sections are omitted from the main dashboard.
26. **Reporting center.** Twelve routes: constraints, ideas, experiments, learnings, funnel, revenue, channels, concentration, loops, readiness, capacity and decisions. Layout follows purpose: evidence narrative, portfolio table, learning statements, stage analysis, scenario sequence, demand bars, loop structure, matrix or gap analysis. Filters are temporary view state and reset on route changes; they do not modify business records.
27. **Executive report.** Presentation-first view with the management focus, health, constraints, evidence, revenue, channels, candidates, risks and 3S context. Detailed tables and controls are reduced. Print styles are provided; no PDF-export service was added.
28. **Visual decisions.** Navy/gold/neutral palette, existing Vazirmatn and RTL, compact header, strong management-focus panel, restrained bars and chronological numeric strips. A trend is labeled with dates/coverage/scenario where relevant. Dense tables turn into labeled records at smaller widths. The existing global Scale header is compact only on dashboard/report routes.
29. **Responsive QA.** Inspected 1440, 1280, 768 and 390px. Found and fixed inherited 600px table minimum width. Final mobile tables measured 311px within a 311px parent, without internal horizontal overflow. Tablet document width 753px within a 768px viewport. Screenshots: `build5f-desktop.jpg`, `build5f-mobile.jpg`.
30. **Performance.** Synthetic test-only datasets cover 20 constraints, 100 ideas, 100 experiments, 20 funnel periods, 20 channels with 24 records each, 15 loops and 20 candidates with 40 capacity areas, plus a doubled dataset. Latest complete-suite measurements were 77ms and 116ms for dashboard aggregation. These are local synthetic measurements, not a production SLA or proof for unlimited data.
31. **Tests and browser results.** All 520 tests passed, including 23 new tests covering scenarios A–V and missing numeric inputs. TypeScript and production build passed. All 15 dashboard/report URLs returned HTTP 200; 18 referenced page assets loaded successfully. All twelve focused reports and the executive report were opened in the browser. Result and capacity-gate filters, saved scenario, source click-through, strategy destination, deep-route refresh and early-stage rendering were checked. Console error/warning inspection was empty. No hydration error was observed.
32. **Dependencies.** None added. Existing Next.js/React, local stores, CSS and node test runner are reused.
33. **Issues and limits.** Fixed wrong initial strategy URL, saved-scenario target interpretation, repeated attention entries, incompatible trend comparisons, ineffective report filters, filtered preparation scope and responsive table overflow. One initial test fixture lacked a current revenue model; the fixture was corrected. Historical charts use stored observations/snapshots, but the dashboard is a current view with selected historical context, not a historical snapshot of the whole company. Existing 5E evidence strings can still contain English enum labels. Some portfolio summaries intentionally remain global when detail filters apply, with scope disclosed. Native PDF export, persisted report presets and arbitrary global as-of reporting are not included.
34. **Before closing SCALE v1.** Review the complete 5A–5F journey with representative company data, confirm business ownership of the documented scoring thresholds and reporting vocabulary, and assess accessibility/print output across target browsers. Decide backup/export and multi-user requirements separately. AI advice, forecasting, external analytics, advanced attribution/CAC/LTV, authentication, billing and backend work remain outside this build.
